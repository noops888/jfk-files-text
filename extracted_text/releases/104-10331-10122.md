SUBJECT:

DO AUGMENTATION OF CSI STAFF

1. The Directorate of Operations will augment the CSI

Historical Review Group with up to eight staff officers-to

support the declassification and review of records relatedito the

John F. Kennedy Assassination Review Board.

These officers will also respond to requirements for

review of other covert action declassification projects

undertaken by CSI.

3.

I understand that CSI will provide office space and

equipment for these officers as appropriations permit.
