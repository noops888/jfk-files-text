Dear David:

This is to acknowledge your letter of 7 November 1995

to Director of Central Intelligence Deutch describing the

Assassination Records Review Board's recent formal deter-.

minations with respect to CIA records.

The appropriate Agency components have again reviewed

the 198 documents

which the Board has decided to release,

either fully or with some information postponed. With one

potential exception, the Agency does not plan to appeal to

the President for additional postponements with respect to

the documents listed in your letter.

The potential

exception is document no. 104-10017-10015.

As we discussed,

the Board,

because of a technical error on our part, made a

determination on this document without having full

information available.

We plan to withhold judgement on

this document pending further review by the Board.

We understand that further review will be needed this

month to determine if additional names in a number of the

documents can be released.

We would be pleased to discuss

with the Board and your staff any issues that may arise with

regard to declassifying and releasing CIA-originated

information.
